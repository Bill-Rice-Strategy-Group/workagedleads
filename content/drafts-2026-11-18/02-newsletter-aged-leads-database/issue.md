**Subject:** You didn't buy leads. You bought a database.

**Preview text:** How to let a $20 AI assistant work your aged leads like past clients.

## Intro

I get the same email a few times a month. Someone bought aged leads, called them for two weeks, and wants to know why they "don't work."

They expected the leads to act like leads. Aged leads don't. These people asked about a product like yours a while back, and they gave their consent to the company that collected the lead, not to you. So you can't text them or put them on an autodialer.

I've always thought of aged leads as something else: a customer database you can buy for about a dollar a name. You work it the way you'd work your past clients. You email them, send them something useful, and call the ones who write back.

The hard part used to be writing a personal email to a thousand people. Now a $20 AI assistant connected to your Google Sheet and your Gmail can do it for you. This week's article walks through the whole setup, step by step, with no technical skills needed.

## Quick Tips From the Trenches

### 1. Read the first 10 emails yourself

Before you let an AI send anything, read its first 10 drafts. Check two things: does it sound like you, and is every fact true? AI will sometimes write "as we discussed" or make up a rate. Tell it plainly never to do either, and keep the setting on that asks your approval before each email goes out.

### 2. Start at 25 emails a day

A sudden burst of hundreds of emails from your account looks like spam to Google, even if each one is personal. Send 20 to 25 a day for the first week or two, then raise it a little each week while replies come in and nothing bounces. Free Gmail caps you around 500 recipients a day. Stay far below that.

### 3. Replies are the signal

Plain Gmail doesn't show you who opened or clicked. That's fine. A reply is the strongest signal you'll get. Each morning, ask the AI to list everyone who replied, sum up what they said, and rank who to call first. Anyone who says "stop" gets marked and never contacted again.

## Industry Insight

**Both big AI assistants can now send email from your Gmail**

In 2026, both Claude and ChatGPT added the ability to send email from a connected Gmail account on their paid plans, not only read it. By default, both ask you to approve each email before it goes out. That's what makes a personal, one-at-a-time email program possible for a solo agent without new software. The approval step also keeps a person in charge of every message, which is how it should stay.
