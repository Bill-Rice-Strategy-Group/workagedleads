## Post

I get the same email a few times a month.

"I bought aged leads. Called them for two weeks. They don't work."

They expected aged leads to act like fresh leads. They won't.

An aged lead asked about a mortgage, a policy, a new roof months ago. Their consent went to whoever collected the lead, not to you. So no texting. No autodialer.

I've always thought of them differently: a customer database you can buy for about a dollar a name.

Work it like your past clients. Send a personal note. Send something useful every month. Call the people who write back.

The old problem was time. Nobody can write 1,000 personal emails.

That changed this year. A $20 Claude or ChatGPT plan can now connect to your Google Sheet and your Gmail. You give it a job description like you would a sales assistant. It sorts your list into groups, writes each person a short, personal email, and you approve every one before it goes.

Add a free booking link so people can pick their own time.

Not a volume play. About 25 emails a day, the pace you'd keep by hand. The AI does the boring part. You take the calls.

I wrote up the whole setup, step by step, for people who aren't technical. Link in the first comment.
